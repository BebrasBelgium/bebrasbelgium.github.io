# Results

The Bebras competition - edition 2024 - took place from **Tuesday, November 12** to **Friday, November 22, 2024**, for the thirteenth time in Belgium. The competition was organized [online](https://bebras.ugent.be/) only.
**5652 students** participated.

Below you will find the top 15 participants in each age category.
